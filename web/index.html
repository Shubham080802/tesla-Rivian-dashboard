    Checks run on every refresh

      What is not here, and why

      • Customer reviews and ratings. No open dataset exists for Tesla or BYD.
      • BYD monthly volumes before 2022, and the passenger and overseas split before 2023. 2022 totals are included and reconcile to BYD's reported 1,863,494 for the year; earlier months and the 2022 split are on the roadmap.
      • Stock prices. No clean free monthly source confirmed yet.
      • Registrations by country. Tesla's China domestic and export figures are included (CPCA). Europe runs to August 2026 (BYD not reported separately in early 2024); BYD's own China retail is not published separately; the U.S. is not included.
      • Sales as revenue. Volumes are what the companies report; BYD's monthly figures are unaudited and its revenue includes phone components.

      Data: Tesla 8-K releases and SEC filings; BYD announcements to HKEX and annual reports; Wikimedia page views; U.S. station locator; FRED. Volumes are what the companies report, not revenue. Not investment advice.
