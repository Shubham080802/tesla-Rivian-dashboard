        What is not here, and why

        • Customer satisfaction surveys and reviews. No open dataset exists for Tesla, Rivian or Lucid. The Quality & safety and Sentiment tabs use NHTSA complaints, crash ratings, EPA figures and news tone instead.
        • Owners' personal demographics. No source publishes buyers' age or income. The Who buys tab describes the neighbourhoods where cars are registered in California, Washington, New York and Connecticut, from the Census Bureau.
        • Companies listed outside the U.S. They are out of scope, so every company here trades on a U.S. exchange, reports to the SEC in US dollars and follows the same accounting rules.
        • Raw share prices. No free source clearly allows republishing prices, so only derived yearly percentage changes are shown on the Stock vs business tab; the monthly prices themselves stay on the maintainer's machine.
        • Registrations by country. Tesla's China domestic and export figures are included (CPCA). Europe runs to August 2026. Rivian sells almost only in North America, so it has no regional split; Tesla's U.S. registrations are not public monthly.
        • Monthly volumes. All three companies report deliveries quarterly, so the forecasts work in quarters.

        Data: Tesla, Rivian and Lucid 8-K releases and SEC filings; Cox Automotive; NHTSA; EPA; state DMVs; Census ACS; GDELT; CPCA; ACEA; Wikimedia page views; U.S. station locator; FRED. Volumes are what the companies report, not revenue. Not investment advice.
